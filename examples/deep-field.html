Deep Field ​

New title, galaxy map and orbit pictures with new music and button sounds. A look and sound pack: it changes no rules.

Download .carinamod

5.2 MB, 19 files. Built from the repository when this site was built; it checks and imports the same as the folder.

What it shows ​

A graphics and sound pack: new title and galaxy backdrops and music. It changes no rules, so it is switched on in the library, per player.
