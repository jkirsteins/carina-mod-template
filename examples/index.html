Example mods ​

Four small working mods, kept here as the template's mods/ folder and listed in the game's mod catalog (install them from BROWSE in the mod library). Each page has a download of the packed .carinamod; read the files, copy one, change it.

ExampleIdSummary
Choir Prismscarina-team.choir-prismsA compatibility add-on for Prism Optics and Hollow Choir: empires with the Resonance pick start knowing Refractive Optics at the Average start.
Deep Fieldcarina-team.deep-fieldNew title, galaxy map and orbit pictures with new music and button sounds. A look and sound pack: it changes no rules.
Hollow Choircarina-team.hollow-choirA new race of hollow-headed singers with its own pictures, ships and theme, and a signature pick, Resonance.
Prism Opticscarina-team.prism-opticsA prism research line: a beam weapon, a targeting computer and a research lattice, with a balance tweak.
