Hollow Choir ​

A new race of hollow-headed singers with its own pictures, ships and theme, and a signature pick, Resonance.

Download .carinamod

1.8 MB, 48 files. Built from the repository when this site was built; it checks and imports the same as the folder.

What it shows ​

A race with every picture and its theme music made for it (no art base), a signature pick called Resonance, and a weapon only empires that take that pick can research.
