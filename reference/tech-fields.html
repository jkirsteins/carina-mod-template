tech-fields ​

A research field: one step of one of the eight research areas. A new field is linked into its area's chain with a splice patch row after the field it follows.

A mod adds records in records/tech-fields/<file>.json ({ "$schema": "carina-records/1", "catalog": "tech-fields", "records": [...] }) and changes base records with patch rows on catalog tech-fields. Schema: tech-fields records schema.

Fields ​

A mod record leaves out id and prov: the game assigns ids, and prov is the project's own provenance.

FieldTypeRequiredNotes
key#<namespace>.<snake_case>: your package id with - read as _, then a nameyes
kind#one of starting, normal, xenon, hyperAdvancedyes
area#one of biology, chemistry, computers, construction, forceFields, physics, power, sociology or nullyesthe research area the field belongs to
prev#key of a record in tech-fields, or nullyesthe field before it (written by the splice row; null in the record)
next#key of a record in tech-fields, or nullyesthe field after it (written by the splice row; null in the record)
cost#integer >= 0yesresearch points to learn the field
level#integer 0..22yesthe field's place in its area; between the neighbours it is spliced between
nameKey#text keyyes

References to other records are written as keys; the game turns them into ids.

Also register it in ​

  • patches: a splice row (key = your field, after = the field it follows) links it into the chain; its level must lie between its neighbours'

Computer players ​

The computer players research a new field like any other; they value it by the aiGroup of its applications.

Limits ​

  • Eight research areas, no ninth area.
  • Only normal fields join a chain, and never after the chain's hyper-advanced end (splice-end).
  • Write prev and next as null in the record; the splice row sets them.
  • level is 0 to 22 (the research value tables).
  • A remove row takes a normal base field out of its chain; its neighbours link to each other. Its applications and everything naming them must go too: the compile lists them (removed-dependent). Starting, Xenon and hyper-advanced fields are not removed.

Patch paths ​

Rows on this catalog address a record by key and a field by path:

  • set: area, cost, kind, level, nameKey, next, prev
  • append, insertAfter, removeValue: none (no list fields)
