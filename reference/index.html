Mod reference ​

What a Phase 1 mod can change, generated from the game's own catalog definitions so it matches the game version it came with. The getting-started guide is the template's README.

Things a mod can add ​

CatalogWhat
applicationsOne application of a research field: what the player picks when the field completes
armorAn armour tier, unlocked by an application (app).
buildingsA colony building
computersA battle computer tier, unlocked by an application (app).
race-picksA race pick: a trait level or a special ability, made of race effects.
racesA playable race: picks, government, homeworld, AI personality tables, text keys and art
shieldsA shield tier, unlocked by an application (app)
specialsA ship special system, unlocked by an application (app)
tech-fieldsA research field: one step of one of the eight research areas
weaponsA ship weapon

More ​

  • Rules catalogs: every number the rules use, changed with patch rows.
  • Patch operations: set, append, insertAfter, removeValue, remove, splice; expect and optional.
  • Effects: the effect kinds buildings, governments, race picks and colony rules combine.
  • Limits: text, ids, package size.
  • Roles: every picture and sound role a mod may supply, with its kind, exact size and frame grammar.
