Limits ​

Content ​

  • Text shown in the game uses printable Latin-1 (the bitmap fonts have no other glyphs): mod titles, author names and every text string.
  • Catalog strings (keys, text keys) are printable ASCII.
  • Numbers are integers; fractions are { num, den } records.
  • At most four applications per research field (the research screen shows four choices).
  • Field levels are 0 to 22 and AI groups 0 to 41 (the research value tables).
  • The custom race screen shows 22 special picks a page; with more, its third column pages.
  • New hulls, governments, drives and fuel cells are not possible: those catalogs are closed (their records are patchable). Their behaviour is tied to the hull sizes, the government family and the movement tables.
  • Mods never write numeric ids: records get ids from the mod's own id block, and references between records use keys.
  • Record keys are <namespace>.<snake_case>: the namespace is the package id with - read as _ (yourname.my-mod -> yourname.my_mod.prism_lance).

Text, art and audio ​

  • Text: i18n/en/<file>.json maps keys to ICU messages; other locales are reported and not used yet. A new key holds the package namespace as dotted segments (ui.yourname.my_mod.hello).
  • A mod may override base text, except proper names (race.*, names.*, tech.field.*.name, tech.app.*.name, ldr.*.name). An override uses only the base message's arguments, with the same kind; a new key takes none, except the ruler name pools (names.rulers.*: i, sex).
  • Messages: printable Latin-1 without the soft hyphen, no tags, dates or times, an other arm in every plural and select, at most 2000 characters, nesting 4 and 32 arms; at most 4000 messages per package.
  • Art and audio: assets/roles.json ("$schema": "carina-roles/1") with roles ({ "role", "file", "frames"? }), races ({ "race", "artBase" }) and artFrom ({ "catalog": "buildings", "key", "from" }: a new building drawn as a base one).
  • A role is a base role (an override), a picture of a race the package adds, or a new role named <namespace>.<name>. Images have the base role's kind and exact size; picture atlases too.
  • A combat ship atlas (ships.combat.*, shipsai.combat.*) brings its frames: <key>.h<0-4>.f<0-3>.x<X>.y<Y>, every unit key and heading at frame 0, each rect inside the image.
  • A building you add can have its own colony lot picture: the atlas role colony.lot.<building key with _ replaced by -> (for acme.tools.big_lab: colony.lot.acme.tools.big-lab), listed with 36 frames, one per cell of the 6 x 6 lot, named r<0-5>c<0-5>_x<X>_y<Y> (X, Y: the screen position of the sprite's top-left pixel, as the base lot atlases do). Each sprite stands on its cell: its screen rect lies inside the cell's box (the cell's four grid corners, 2 px margin, room above for a building 1.15 cell widths high), frames do not overlap in the image, and the cell boxes are listed in src/content/presentation/building-art.ts. A building drawn as a base one (artFrom) must not also list its own lot role (art-from-twice). A building with neither picture is not drawn on the lot.
  • Audio: music.cue.<id> and sfx.<nnn> as Ogg (Opus or Vorbis), with an optional <role>.m4a twin. Music at most 300 s, effects at most 10 s, by the file's own length.
  • Packages stack in load order: a later package's role or text replaces an earlier one's. Text and art are not part of the rules hash.

Catalogs ​

  • Open to new records: applications, armor, buildings, computers, race-picks, races, shields, specials, tech-fields, weapons.
  • Closed (patch only): ai-objectives, ai-personality, ai-tuning, assimilation-rules, audience-rules, build-lists, cheat-codes, colonize-rules, colony-economy, combat-rules, council-rules, difficulty, diplomacy-rules, drives, espionage-rules, event-rules, events, finance-rules, fuel-cells, galaxy-gen, governments, governor-policies, history-rules, hulls, leader-rules, leaders, message-rules, monster-rules, monsters, movement, planet-mod-rules, planet-types, population-rules, production-rules, race-rules, relations-matrix, research, score, settings, ship-design, ship-lifecycle, star-types, start-state, transport-rules, troop-units, vaelori-designs, vaelori-rules, weapon-mods.
  • Ids: base records stay below 1024; each mod that adds records gets one block of 256 ids per catalog (at most 60 blocks in a game), so at most 256 new records per catalog per mod.

Package ​

  • Zip at most 32 MiB, 2000 entries, 128 MiB unpacked; rules files (records, patches, migrations) at most 4 MiB in all.
  • One JSON file at most 2 MiB, nested at most 32 deep, 200000 values; mod.json at most 64 KiB.
  • Paths: ASCII letters, digits, ., _, -; at most 200 bytes and 8 segments; no two paths that differ only by case.
  • Images: PNG or WebP, at most 4096 pixels a side, 16 Mpx each and 64 Mpx in all (1 Mpx = 1048576 pixels). Audio: Ogg (and m4a).
  • Folders for later formats are refused with needs-newer-game: rules/, scenarios/, campaigns/, ui/, scripts/.
  • No JavaScript, HTML or other code: a package is data only.
