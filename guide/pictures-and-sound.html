Pictures and sound ​

This page covers how a mod supplies pictures and sound: roles, file formats and sizes.

Roles ​

assets/roles.json connects your files to the places the game draws and plays them. Its "$schema" is "carina-roles/1". Each row of roles is { "role": "<role id>", "file": "assets/<path>" }:

json
{
  "role": "menus.title.backdrop",
  "file": "assets/title.png"
}

A role is a place in the game: a title backdrop, a galaxy star field, a race portrait, a sound effect, a music cue. Roles lists every role with its kind, exact size and frame grammar. A file of the wrong size or kind is refused on import, with the role named.

A role is one of three things:

  • A base role, which the mod overrides.
  • A picture of a race the package adds.
  • A new role named <namespace>.<name>.

Packages stack in load order. A later package's role or text replaces an earlier one's.

Files ​

  • Pictures are PNG or WebP. Each image is the exact size of its role. At most 4096 pixels a side, 16 Mpx per image and 64 Mpx per package.
  • Sound is Ogg, Opus or Vorbis. Music is at most 300 seconds and an effect at most 10, by the file's own length.
  • An .m4a twin is optional: the same role with .m4a added, for browsers that do not play Ogg. One case requires it: a race without an art base must bring music.race.<race key> and its .m4a twin.

Races ​

A race you add either names an art base or supplies its whole contract.

  • With an art base, { "race": "<your race key>", "artBase": "<stock race key>" } in races, every picture the race does not bring is the base race's.
  • Without one, the race brings every role of its contract: portraits, ambassador, emblems, council medallions, ships, colonist figures and theme music. Roles lists them. Hollow Choir does this.

The numbered portrait variants of the stock Humans are not available to a race you add. The importer refuses them with role-namespace.

Buildings ​

A building you add is drawn on the colony lot in one of two ways, and not both:

  • Its own atlas, the role colony.lot.<building key with each _ written as ->. It has 36 sprites, one per cell of the 6 x 6 lot, named r<0-5>c<0-5>_x<X>_y<Y> in the frames list of the role row. Roles: building lot art gives the grammar and the screen box of every cell. Prism Optics is the worked example.
  • artFrom, which draws it like a base building: { "catalog": "buildings", "key": "<your building>", "from": "<base building key>" }.

Listing both is refused with art-from-twice. A building with neither is not drawn on the lot.

Cosmetic packs ​

A package that holds only text, pictures or sound has no rules capability. It is a cosmetic pack.

  • It changes nothing about the rules, so players need no agreement on it.
  • On the INSTALLED tab it sits under LOOK AND SOUND and has an ON/OFF switch in its row.
  • It does not appear in the RULES row of New Game.
  • Its text wins over a rules mod's text.

Deep Field is a cosmetic pack with backdrops and music.

Limits ​

Limits lists the bounds: sizes, paths and the combat ship atlas frame names.
